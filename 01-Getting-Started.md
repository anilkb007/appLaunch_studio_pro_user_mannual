# Getting Started - Anil

This page tours the main window and then takes you from an empty app to an
exported App Store screenshot.

## The window at a glance

The main window has three columns and an inspector.

| Area | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Folders** (left) | Your apps. Select an app to work on its App Store version; expand it for its custom product pages. The **App Store** section at the bottom is the App Store Connect browser. |
| **Navigator** (middle) | The frames of the selected app or page. A *frame* is one saved design: a device, a layout, a background, text, and the screenshots inside it. |
| **Canvas** (centre) | A live preview of the selected frame at its export size. What you see here is exactly what gets exported. |
| **Inspector** (right) | Every setting for the selected frame, split into a **Design** tab and a **Localization** tab. |

Above the canvas you will find the **Move** toggle, a single/gallery switch
(when a frame has more than one screenshot), **Templates**, the translate
button, the **Language** pop-up that chooses the preview language, and the
button that hides or shows the inspector.

## Your first screenshot

::: steps

### Create an app

Click **+** at the top of the Folders sidebar. In the **New App** sheet give
the app a name, optionally **Choose App Icon**, and pick the default frames:
**By platform** creates one frame for each platform you switch on;
**All App Store sizes** creates one frame for every screenshot size App Store
Connect asks for. Click **Create**.

### Pick a frame

The Navigator lists the frames that were just created. Select one — the row
shows the device, its size and colour, for example *6.9″ · Deep Blue*. To add
another, click **+** above the Navigator and choose a platform and device in
the **New Frame** sheet.

### Add a screenshot

Drag a PNG or JPEG from the Finder anywhere onto the window, or open the
**Media** card in the inspector and click **Add Screenshot…**. The image is
fitted into the device's screen automatically.

### Design it

In the inspector, choose a **Background** (solid, linear or radial gradient,
or one of the presets), then type a **Title** and **Subtitle** in the
**Text** card. Use the **Device Frame** card to change device, size and
position, or switch **3D frame** on for a rendered device you can rotate.

### Export

Open the **Export** card, pick a resolution and click **Export Current** to
save a PNG, or **Export All** to write every screenshot of the frame into a
folder. The file is rendered at exactly the size shown on the canvas badge.

:::
