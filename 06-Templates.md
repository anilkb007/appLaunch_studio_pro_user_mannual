# Templates

A template stores a complete design — device layout, background, and the
title and subtitle in every language — so you can reuse it on other frames.

## Applying a template

1. Select a frame and click **Templates** above the canvas.
2. Filter with the sidebar: **All Templates**, macOS, iPhone or iPad.
3. Select a template and click **Apply**, or double-click it.

The frame keeps its own device but takes over the template's layout,
background and text. If the template has more screens than the frame, empty
screens are added to match.

Built-in templates: **Mac — Hero**, **Mac — Dark Minimal**, **iPhone — Bold
Headline** and **iPhone — Tilted Pair**.

> Applying a template replaces the current title and subtitle **including
> their translations**. Duplicate the frame first if you want to keep the
> original.

## Saving your own

Once a frame looks the way you want, open the gallery and click **Save
Current as Template**. It appears in the gallery under its platform, and you
can remove it later with **Delete Template** in its right-click menu.
Built-in templates cannot be deleted.

## Free plan limits

The free plan includes two template applies; saving templates needs
**Pro**. A caption in the gallery shows how many applies remain.
