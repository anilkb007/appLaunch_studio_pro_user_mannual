# Localization

Every title and subtitle can exist in each of the 50 App Store languages.
The design stays the same; only the words change — and the app can write
most of them for you.

## Languages

Six languages are enabled when you create a frame: English (US), Chinese
(Simplified), Spanish (Spain), Japanese, German and French. English (US) is
the **base language** that translations start from.

Open the inspector's **Localization** tab to manage them:

- **Add Language…** enables another App Store locale.
- Each language row has a **Translate from English** button, a remove button,
  and its own **Title** and **Subtitle** fields.
- **Show All** / **Show Fewer** expands or collapses the list, and **Search
  language or text** filters it.
- **Auto-translate all** fills every enabled language in one go.

The **Language** pop-up above the canvas chooses which language the canvas
previews; the **Text** card in the Design tab edits that language directly.

## On-device translation

Translation runs on your Mac using **Apple Intelligence** — nothing is sent
to a server and no account or API key is needed.

- It requires macOS 26 with Apple Intelligence turned on in **System
  Settings**.
- Apple Intelligence covers about 29 of the App Store languages. Languages it
  does not know are left blank for you to type in.
- Always read the result. Machine translations of very short marketing lines
  can miss the tone you want.

If Apple Intelligence is unavailable the Localization tab says so, and you
can still enter every translation by hand.

## The Localized Editor window

For frames with many screenshots and many languages, the spreadsheet view is
faster. Click the grid button next to the **Localization** header to open the
**Localized Editor** in its own resizable window.

- Rows are each screenshot's or recording's **Title** and **Subtitle**;
  columns are languages.
- Use the device menu and **Show All Devices** / **Show One Device** to widen
  or narrow the view.
- **Translate All** fills the whole grid; the sparkle buttons on a column
  header or row translate just that language or that line.
- Tick the circles on column headers to select languages, then **Delete (n)**
  removes them from the frame.
- **Add Language…**, **Show All Languages** / **Show Fewer** and **Done**
  work as in the inspector.
