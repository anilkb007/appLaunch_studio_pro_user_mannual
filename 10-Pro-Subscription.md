# Pro Subscription

App Launch Studio Pro is free to try with real exports. A **Pro**
subscription removes the limits and unlocks the publishing workflow.

## What is included

| Feature | Free | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Apps | 1 | Unlimited |
| Frames per app | 1 | Unlimited |
| Screenshots per frame | 1 | Unlimited |
| Extra device frames (Frame 2 and 3) | — | Yes |
| Recordings and video export | — | Yes |
| 3D device frames | — | Yes |
| Template applies | 2 | Unlimited |
| Saving templates | — | Yes |
| Auto-translate all | 2 runs | Unlimited |
| Single-language translations | 10 | Unlimited |
| Screenshot exports | 2 per hour, 4 in total | Unlimited |
| Localized export | — | Yes |
| App Store Connect setup, upload and custom product pages | — | Yes |

Captions beginning *Free plan:* in the inspector show how much of each free
allowance is left.

## Subscribing

Choose **Subscribe to Pro…** from the app menu, or click any locked feature.
The store sheet offers **Annual**, **Monthly** and **Weekly** plans; the
annual plan can optionally be paid monthly. Purchases go through your Apple
ID with the App Store's usual confirmation, and features unlock as soon as the
sheet closes.

Once subscribed, the sheet shows *Pro active until* with your renewal date.

## Restoring a purchase

On a new Mac, or if features appear locked after reinstalling, open
**Subscribe to Pro…** and click **Restore Purchases**. The app also
re-checks your subscription every time it becomes active, so a purchase made
on another Mac signed into the same Apple ID is picked up automatically.

## Managing or cancelling

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open **System Settings ▸ Apple Account ▸
Media & Purchases ▸ Subscriptions** (or the App Store app's account page) to
change plan or cancel. Pro features stay available until the end of the paid
period.
