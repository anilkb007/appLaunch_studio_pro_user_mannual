# Media

*Media* is what goes inside the device: screenshots, screen recordings, or
empty placeholder screens. Each media item becomes one exported image (or
video), and every item in a frame shares the frame's device, background and
text settings unless you change them per item.

## Adding screenshots

- **Drag and drop** a PNG or JPEG from the Finder anywhere onto the window.
- Or open the **Media** card in the inspector and click **Add Screenshot…**.
- **Add Empty Screen** adds a placeholder you can fill later with
  **Choose…**, or swap an existing image with **Replace…**.

Images are fitted into the device's screen with aspect-fill cropping, so a
screenshot taken on the matching device lines up exactly. Imported files are
copied into the app's own media folder.

> The free plan allows one screenshot per frame. Additional screenshots need
> **Pro** — see *Pro Subscription*.

## Recordings (app previews)

Click **Add Recording…** to import an MP4 or QuickTime movie. Recordings play
inside the device on the canvas, with play, pause and restart controls in the
Media list.

- Up to **3 recordings** per frame.
- App Store app previews must be **15 to 30 seconds** long. Shorter clips
  show a warning at export time; longer ones cannot be exported.
- The device frame cannot be rotated for the recording slot.
- Recordings are a **Pro** feature.

## Browsing several items

When a frame has more than one media item:

- The canvas shows **‹ n / N ›** arrows to step through them.
- The single/gallery switch above the canvas shows all items side by side,
  each captioned with its file name.
- Most inspector cards offer **Apply to all screenshots** (or **Apply to all
  media**) so a change made on one item can be pushed to the rest.

## Clearing media

**Clear All Screenshots** in the Media card removes every item from the
frame at once. Like other delete actions it takes effect immediately.

## Panorama (iPhone)

A panorama is one wide image spread across all of a frame's screenshots, so
the App Store gallery reads as a single continuous picture as the customer
swipes.

1. In a frame with several screenshots, open the **Panorama** card.
2. Click **Load Image…** and pick a wide image.
3. Switch the panorama on. Each screenshot now shows its slice of the image
   behind the device; the regular background is hidden while the panorama is
   active.

Use **Replace Image…** to swap the picture or **Remove** to go back to the
normal background. The Panorama card only appears for iPhone frames.
